AIRMASS RUNTIME WARNINGS IN L1.0 (2026-11-05)

Seen in the top-level `make -j6`, during this campaign's L1.0 stage:

    seastar_atmosphere.py:42: RuntimeWarning: invalid value encountered ...   (airmass_rayleigh)
    seastar_atmosphere.py:47: RuntimeWarning: invalid value encountered ...   (airmass_aerosol)

The make still exits 0. The warnings are not a problem with the airmass formulas: they come from a
bad position in the first timestep of a run. Nothing has been fixed yet.


WHAT HAPPENS

Kasten & Young has (96.07995 - zenith) ** -1.6364 and Kasten has (93.885 - zenith) ** -1.253. Past
those zeniths the base is negative, a negative number to a fractional power is nan, and numpy warns.

Here the zenith is 121 degrees, in timestep 0 of SeaSTAR_2026-09-25_14-32-41: sun_elev -31.05 in the
middle of the day, from lat 7.047, lon -24.128. The instrument was at 35.235, -120.641.

Cause, checked against the samples in the L05 for that timestep:

- The GPS had no fix yet when the logging started, so the first samples read 0, not nan. Of the 60
  samples, 10 are nan, 40 are 0 and 10 are 35.235.
- L05-to-L06_sun.py (lines 83-85) takes np.nanmean of imu_press, imu_lat and imu_lon, so the zeros are
  averaged in: 35.235 * 10/50 = 7.047, and the same for lon.
- L06-to-L10_sun.py only treats a position as a dropout if it is exactly 0, so a part-zero average
  counts as good. It isn't filled or given AOD_POSITION_FILLED, and it goes into solar_position.
- imu_press is averaged down the same way (20115 Pa against 100574 Pa). That one is caught, by the
  IMU_PRESS_MIN/MAX bounds, which is probably why nearly every run reports 1 or 2 pressures filled.


HOW FAR IT GOES

Timesteps whose position is a part-zero average, in the 20260922 to 20260930 campaigns (sun_elev is
from the bad position, sun_ephem_elev is the tracker's):

2026-09-29_11-58-42 and 2026-09-30_07-08-47 have none. Where two timesteps are listed, timestep 0 is
all zeros, so it is filled, and np.interp copies the bad position of timestep 1 into it.

Only 14-32-41 gives a nan, because only there is the zenith past the limits. The others have a wrong
sun position and airmass but no warning.

These were found by comparing each position with a 41-timestep rolling median, looking for lat and
lon reduced by the same fraction, below 0.995. A timestep with only a few zero samples would be missed.
No part-zero timesteps turned up later in any run, only at the start.


EFFECT ON THE RESULTS

None on the unflagged data so far. All of these timesteps have AOD_TRACKING set, so they drop out of
both the all-flags screening and the tracking-only AOD plots. That is by luck, though: nothing flags
the bad position itself, and a run starting with good tracking and a late GPS fix would get through.

It does not explain why 2026-09-25_11-56-10 has 0 of 194 timesteps unflagged. Only one of its
timesteps is affected.


POSSIBLE FIX

In L05-to-L06_sun.py, leave the zero samples out of the means of imu_lat and imu_lon, and imu_press,
so a timestep averages only its real samples. A timestep with no real samples then needs to stay
detectable by the good_position test in L06-to-L10_sun.py, which looks for exactly 0. Each campaign
has its own copy of the script.
